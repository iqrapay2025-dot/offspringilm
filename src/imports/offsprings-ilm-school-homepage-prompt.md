# Figma Make Prompt — Offsprings Ilm School Homepage (Pitch Concept)

## Style Reference
Replicate the layout and structure of the **Finovate consulting template** (dark forest-green + lime-green accent, editorial sans-serif typography, generous whitespace, card-based sections) — but reskinned for a private Islamic K-12 school in Ilorin, Nigeria. Swap the corporate/finance tone for warm, credible, education-and-values tone. Keep the confident, modern layout bones; change the soul.

---

## Global Design Tokens

- **Primary dark:** Deep forest green `#173A2E` (used for dark section backgrounds, footer, header text)
- **Accent (CTA/highlights):** Lime green `#C6F135` (buttons, small tags, underlines, icon badges)
- **Secondary accent:** Warm gold `#D9A441` (Islamic/heritage touch — used sparingly on icons or dividers)
- **Background neutral:** Off-white `#F7F6F2`
- **Text dark:** Near-black `#1B1B1B`
- **Text on dark:** `#F7F6F2` / muted lime-white for secondary text
- **Font — Display/Headings:** A rounded-but-confident serif or soft-geometric sans (e.g., "Fraunces" or "Poppins SemiBold") for headline warmth
- **Font — Body:** Clean sans-serif (e.g., "Inter" or "Work Sans")
- **Shape language:** Rounded-corner cards (16–20px radius), pill-shaped buttons, small circular icon badges like the Finovate reference
- **Imagery:** Real-feeling photos of Nigerian schoolchildren in uniform, classroom moments, group prayer/assembly, teachers engaging with students — warm, natural light, not stock-corporate

## Notes for Figma Make
- Keep the Finovate reference's generous section padding and card shadow style (soft, low-opacity shadows, not heavy).
- Icon badges should be small lime-green circles with a simple line icon inside (education/Islamic-adjacent icons: book, moon/star subtle accent, handshake, graduation cap).
- Avoid stock "corporate finance" imagery — replace every photo slot with a school/classroom/students scenario as described above.
- Keep overall structure 1:1 with Finovate's rhythm (hero → 3-card intro → 4-icon services → dark philosophy band → content section → CTA band → footer) since that rhythm is what's being pitched as the visual system.
